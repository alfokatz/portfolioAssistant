## en-US

```
NAME
Porty: your portfolio with AI

SHORT
Your portfolio in one place, and Porty explains why your holdings moved.

FULL
Porty brings your investments together in one place and explains them in plain words. No jargon, no screens full of numbers: open the app and see how your portfolio is doing today.

YOUR WHOLE PORTFOLIO, IN ONE PLACE
• With Premium, connect eToro, Binance and other brokers in read-only mode: your positions update on their own.
• Your broker isn't listed? Upload a statement (CSV, Excel or PDF) or a screenshot and Porty loads it for you.
• You can also add your purchases by hand or tell Porty in the chat: it only records them, it doesn't trade for you.
• See your total value, how much it went up or down today, and your portfolio chart over time.

PORTY, YOUR ASSISTANT
• Ask about your portfolio: "How am I doing this month?", "Why did my holdings drop?".
• It tells you what happened with the companies you own, with links to the original articles (Premium).
• It flags what's easy to miss: if a position weighs too much or, with Premium, if your portfolio has drifted from your plan.
• On Saturdays it leaves you your weekly report (full version in Gold).
• Porty's answers are generated by AI and may contain errors: double-check important data.

YOUR PLAN AND YOUR GOALS (PREMIUM)
• Build your portfolio plan: how much you want to hold of each thing. Porty lets you know when you drift away.
• Set savings goals and see how much more you need to contribute to reach them.

STOCK WATCHLIST
• Follow stocks you're interested in even if you don't own them: up to 3 on Free, 30 on Premium and 100 on Gold.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
• With the widget you see today's % and your portfolio news without opening the app. You can hide your positions if you prefer.

PLANS
• Free: your portfolio with Porty, up to 10 positions, 3 stocks on your watchlist and logging transactions by chat.
• Premium: one broker connected that updates on its own, unlimited positions, why your holdings moved, portfolio plan and goals.
• Gold: everything in Premium, plus several connected brokers, analysis of any company, its news explained, quarterly earnings and the full Saturday report.
Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play.

YOUR DATA
• Broker connections are read-only: Porty never trades, holds or moves your money.
• You can delete your account and your data whenever you want, from the app.

Porty provides information to help you understand your investments. It is not a broker or a financial advisor, it doesn't execute orders and it doesn't make buy or sell recommendations. Investing involves risk, and past performance does not guarantee future results.
```
